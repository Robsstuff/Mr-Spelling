About Mr Spelling

Mr Spelling is the coolest teacher in the room: dropping spelling knowledge with the energy of a hip-hop MC. Part educator, part performer, he turns tricky words into unforgettable bars so every student walks away spelling like a champion.

The songs run 60 to 90 seconds, so they fit a starter, a transition, or the last five minutes of a lesson. Every song has a matching worksheet on this site.

Nothing here is behind a login. Play the videos, print the sheets, use them however your class needs.

The Rule

THE SOUND OF THE VOWELS

A vowel can make many different sounds depending on the syllable type it is in and the letters around it. There are five main vowel sounds to know:

Short
ă ĕ ĭ ŏ ŭ

Vowel in a closed syllable. A consonant traps it, making it short.

cat · bed · pig · hot · sun
Long
ā ē ī ō ū

Vowel in an open syllable. Nothing traps it — it says its name.

table · fever · tiger · robot
Schwa
ə

Vowel in an unstressed syllable. Lazy vowel makes the /uh/ sound.

sofa · pencil · lemon · circus
Bossy R
AR ER IR OR UR

The letter R controls the vowel before it, changing its sound.

star · bird · fern · corn · fur
Bossy Y
y → ē / ī

The letter Y acts as a vowel: long E at the end of a long word, long I in a short word.

baby · candy · fly · sky

PLURAL SPELLING RULE 4

When a noun ends in F or FE, you usually cannot just add S to make it plural. Instead, drop the F or FE and add VES. So elf becomes elves, leaf becomes leaves, and knife becomes knives. Watch out for the exceptions: some F words just add S!

Drop F or FE, Add VES

HOW IT WORKS
Plural Rule 4: Nouns Ending in F or FE
word + F → F + VES
word + FE → FE + VES

To make most F/FE nouns plural: 1) Drop the F or FE from the end. 2) Add VES. The F sound changes to a V sound in the plural: leaf → leaves, knife → knives.

Exceptions to Rule 4

WATCH OUT
F words that just add S (don't drop the F)
roofs, beliefs

Some F words just add S without dropping the F: roof → roofs, belief → beliefs, chief → chiefs. These are common exceptions: if unsure, check a dictionary.

doubled F

Nouns ending in a double F (like cliff, staff, bluff) just add S: never drop the FF: cliffs, staffs, bluffs. The doubling rule protects the short vowel; don't break it.

scarves / scarfs

A few nouns have two accepted plural forms: scarves OR scarfs, dwarves OR dwarfs, hooves OR hoofs. Both spellings are correct for these words.

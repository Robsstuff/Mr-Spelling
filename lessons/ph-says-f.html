A digraph is two letters that work together to make one sound. When P and H appear together, they always say /f/. This is different from F alone, but the sound is exactly the same. PH words usually came into English from Greek: words like phone (from "phon" meaning sound), photo (from "phos" meaning light), and alphabet (from the first two Greek letters, alpha and beta). When you see PH in a word, read it as one smooth /f/ sound and do not try to say /p/ and /h/ separately.

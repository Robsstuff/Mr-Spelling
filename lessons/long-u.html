Two Sounds of Long U

Long U is the only vowel with two distinct sounds. It can say /yoo/ (the letter name, as in cube, mule, huge) or /oo/ (as in moon, heard in rule, pool, blue). Both sounds are very common. One reliable guideline from the song: after R or L, long U always says /oo/, never /yoo/. That is why rule, blue and flute say /oo/. But /oo/ is not limited to after R and L. It also appears after other consonants: pool, fruit, crew, rude. When you meet a new long U word, you may need to learn which sound it uses.

Long U

Two Sounds: /yoo/ as in Cube and /oo/ as in Rule

The letter U has two long sounds. It can say /yoo/ (the name of the letter, as in cube, mule, huge) or it can say /oo/ (as in rule, blue, pool). There is a helpful rule: when a long U sound comes right after the letters R or L, it says /oo/, not /yoo/. That is why rule says /oo/ and blue says /oo/, but cube and mule say /yoo/.

Two Sounds of Long U

Long U says /yoo/ normally, but /oo/ after R or L
/yoo/
the letter name
cube, mule, fuse, huge
/oo/
after R or L
rule, blue, flute, crew

Long U is the only vowel with two distinct sounds. Most of the time, long U says /yoo/: it sounds like saying the letter name "U" out loud. You hear it in cube, mule, fuse, huge. But when a long U sound follows the letters R or L directly, it changes to /oo/: the same sound as in moon or soon. You hear it in rule, blue, flute. To check which sound to use, look at the consonant just before the long U: if it is R or L, say /oo/; otherwise say /yoo/.

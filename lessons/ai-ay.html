VOWEL DIGRAPHS

Both AI and AY make the long A sound (like the letter name "A"). The trick is knowing which spelling to use. AI comes at the start or middle of a word. AY comes at the end of a word — because English words rarely end in the letter I.

HOW IT WORKS

The AI / AY Rule

AI at the Start or Middle — AY at the End
AI
start or middle of a word
aim, rain, sail, wait
AY
at the end of a word
pay, play, spray, stay

Both AI and AY make exactly the same long A sound. The position in the word tells you which one to use. AI works at the start or in the middle of a word. AY works at the end — because English words very rarely end in the letter I (the letter Y does the job of final I instead).

WATCH OUT

Exceptions

Words that don't follow the rule
they, grey

These use EY to make the long A sound at the end. Not all words use AY at the end — some use the EY digraph instead. Both are correct for those words; you must learn them.

eight, weight

Some words use EIGH to make the long A sound. This is a less common pattern but appears in several common words: eight, weight, freight, neighbour.

birthday

A compound word: birth + day. The AY is at the end of the word day, which confirms the rule: AY comes at the end. Many compound words ending in –day, –way, –play follow this pattern.
